## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/🚀_HOW_IT_WORKS-000000?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=FF1493" />

</div>

```diff
+ ①  Add the Digital Den 360 manifest URL to your Aurora dashboard
+ ②  Launch Digital Den 360 from your scripts menu
+ ③  Browse the catalog — games, emus, tools
+ ④  Hit download — it installs straight to your console
+ ⑤  Play. No PC required.
```

<div align="center">

> ⚠️ **Requires a jailbroken 360** — RGH, JTAG, or BadUpdate. All fully supported.

</div>

<div align="center">

## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/❓_FAQ-000000?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=FF69B4" />

</div>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Is Digital Den 360 legal?</strong></span></summary>

Yes. Digital Den 360 only hosts **homebrew and open-source content**. No retail games, no XBLA, no piracy. Ever.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Do I need a modded console?</strong></span></summary>

Yes. You need a jailbroken Xbox 360 — either RGH, JTAG, or BadUpdate. **All three are fully supported.** Stock consoles cannot run Digital Den 360.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>When does it launch?</strong></span></summary>

Digital Den 360 is in **Phase 1 (Development)**. Phase 2 (Premium Beta) is coming soon, followed by Phase 3 (Free Release). Star the repo to get notified.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Why pay if the store becomes free?</strong></span></summary>

Premium and Founder get **early access months before everyone else**, plus **exclusive content** that never comes to the free tier. One-time payment — you keep it forever.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Is Premium a subscription?</strong></span></summary>

No. Premium ($5) and Founder ($20) are **one-time payments**. You pay once and keep access forever. The optional Supporter tier ($2/month) is a subscription for those who want to keep supporting development.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>What happens when beta ends?</strong></span></summary>

Premium and Founder keep their tier and badges permanently. The free tier opens with the full standard catalog. Premium users keep their exclusive content on top.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Can I submit my own homebrew?</strong></span></summary>

Absolutely. Submit via GitHub once the den goes live. See the partners page on the website for details.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Is this safe for my console?</strong></span></summary>

Yes. Digital Den 360 only downloads from trusted GitHub Releases. No unknown sources, no sketchy links. Your console stays safe.

</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:#FF1493;"><strong>Who made Digital Den 360?</strong></span></summary>

Digital Den 360 is made by [**Frosty-Dev-Xbox**](https://github.com/Frosty-Dev-Xbox). It's a passion project for the homebrew community.

</details>
